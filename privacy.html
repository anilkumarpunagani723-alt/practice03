Privacy notice

Information about this frontend demo.

Local demo data

The cart and wishlist are saved in your browser's LocalStorage on this device. They are not transmitted to a server. You can remove these values by clearing this site's browser storage.

Forms and payments

Newsletter and contact forms show a local demo notice only; they do not send information. Checkout does not process orders or payments. Do not enter card details or other sensitive payment information on this demo.

Account demo

The sign-in and account creation forms are visual demonstrations only. Login information is not sent to a server or saved by this site. Do not enter a real password.

Third-party resources

This site may load font files from Google Fonts and photography from Unsplash. Those providers may receive standard connection data when your browser requests their resources; consult their privacy information for details.
