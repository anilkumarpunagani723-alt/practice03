Frequently asked questions

Demo storefront information.

Are these products available to buy?

No. This is a frontend demo with sample product listings. No inventory or fulfilment service is connected.

Are prices and ratings real?

No. Prices, discounts, ratings and review counts are illustrative data only.

How does checkout work?

The checkout demonstrates a form and order summary, but does not send an order or take payment. Never enter card details on this demo.

Will a product fit my car?

Do not assume fitment from this demo. Verify the exact part and vehicle compatibility with the product manufacturer or a qualified installer.

Can I contact customer support?

Business contact information is placeholder text. The contact form does not deliver messages without an email or backend integration.

How are delivery and returns handled?

Shipping rates, delivery coverage and return terms have not been configured. A real business must publish verified policies before launch.
